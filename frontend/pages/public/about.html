About FloorballKenya

The official platform powering floorball in Kenya.

Our mission

FloorballKenya exists to grow the sport of floorball across the country — connecting teams, players, leagues, tournaments, and fans in one modern platform. From grassroots youth programs to national championships, we provide the digital infrastructure that the sport needs to thrive.

What we offer

🏑 League Management

Full fixtures, results, live standings, and player statistics — updated automatically.

🛒 Marketplace

Sticks, balls, jerseys, and gear from verified Kenyan vendors, with M-Pesa checkout.

👥 Team & Player Hub

Every team has a profile. Every player has stats. Every match tells a story.

📊 Live Standings

League tables recalculated automatically after every completed match.
